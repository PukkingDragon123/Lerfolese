# Audio credits

Every file in this folder is an edit of a real recording or production by the credited author, found online. Nothing was synthesised for this project. Edits were limited to trimming, fades, loudness normalisation, light filtering, pitch/speed variants, reversing, and layering two or more of the listed recordings.

Licences accepted: CC0 1.0, public domain, CC BY 3.0 and CC BY 4.0. The licence was checked on each source page (or in the licence file inside the pack). Nothing here is NC, ND, Sampling+, unknown, Mixkit or Pixabay. A few files reuse licence-vetted copies from the owner's repositories PukkingDragon123/special-telegram (Kenney Impact Sounds edits) and PukkingDragon123/effective-octo-train (Freesound CC0 recordings). Their original credits are carried over below.

## Processing

- **SFX** (`sfx/`): mono, 44.1 kHz, MP3 CBR 112 kbps. Leading and trailing silence trimmed, short fades, peaks at or below about -1 dBFS. Loudness targets about -17 LUFS (max momentary, 400 ms). Very short transients such as UI clicks, card taps and knocks get at most 6 dB of look-ahead limiting, so they stay quieter than that. The `vol` values in `manifest.json` balance them.
- **Ambience** (`amb/`): stereo, 44.1 kHz, MP3 CBR 112 kbps. Loops are 45-60 s. Layers are levelled before mixing. The tail is crossfaded into the head (3 s, equal-power), so the file loops seamlessly. Loudness is about -24 LUFS integrated. `night` (-25) and `campfire` (-26) are slightly lower because of peak limiting, and their `vol` is raised to match. MP3 adds about 1105 samples of encoder delay at the start, so for sample-accurate looping in Web Audio, set `loopStart` to about 0.025 s.
- **Music** (`music/`): stereo, 44.1 kHz, MP3 LAME VBR V6 (about 105-125 kbps on these tracks, rather than 128, to keep the folder under 35 MB). Loudness is about -18 LUFS integrated (`map` and `battle2` -19.5, held back by their peaks), with peaks at or below -1 dBFS. The incompetech tracks are full tracks, except `menu`, which is trimmed. The stings are normalised to about -17 LUFS, a little louder than the music beds.
- **`vol`** in `manifest.json` comes from each sound's measured loudness, so the categories sit sensibly against each other: UI is soft, cards and paper are moderate, hits and roars are prominent. Music (0.5) and ambience (about 0.5) assume those SFX levels. Treat every `vol` as a starting point for the mix.
